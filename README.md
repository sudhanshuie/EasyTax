# EasyTax 🇳🇱

**A guided tax filing assistant for Indian expats living in the Netherlands.**

EasyTax walks you through every question needed to file your Dutch income tax return — with plain-English explanations, the relevant Dutch law, and exactly where to find each document.

## Who is this for?

Indian citizens living and working in the Netherlands, filing their annual Dutch income tax return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting).

## What it covers

- Box 1: Employment income, 30% ruling, Indian income & treaty relief
- Box 3: Worldwide assets — Dutch savings + Indian NRE/NRO/EPF/mutual funds/property
- Social security: A1 certificates, zorgtoeslag eligibility
- India–Netherlands Double Tax Treaty (DTT) provisions
- Document checklist & filing guide for Belastingdienst

## Legal Notice

EasyTax provides guidance based on publicly available Dutch tax law and the India–Netherlands Tax Treaty. It is not a licensed tax advisory service. Always verify with a registered tax advisor (belastingadviseur) for your specific situation.
